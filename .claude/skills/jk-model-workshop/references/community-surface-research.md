# Community research: model surfaces, materials and export

Use this reference to choose a repair or construction method for a character, player skin, creature or weapon model. It adds original synthesis of 20 further Massassi forum threads, two additional pages of previously cited threads, four model/skin tutorials, and a public Blender-Sith announcement. Research was conducted through headless HTTP and search on 2026-09-30. Forum testimony is valuable diagnostic evidence; it is not a fresh reproduction on every current renderer. Threads 36447 and 41914 also appear in the concurrent [community modeling research](community-modeling-research.md); deduplicate them when combining totals.

## Choose the useful route

| Your situation | Useful option | First small experiment |
|---|---|---|
| A quick skin or familiar-moving character | Preserve a working hierarchy/animation set; repaint or replace selected rigid parts. | Re-export one changed part with a numbered checker and play a familiar animation. |
| Existing indexed MATs turn pink or change color | Recover the intended CMP and inspect the palette-dependent conversion. | Decode the same MAT with the intended palette and a representative alternate palette. |
| The right pictures appear on the wrong body parts | Repair material-slot/per-face index mapping. | Compare material names and face references before and after assembly. |
| UVs change during an old tool chain | Use a UV-capable modeler and minimize conversion steps; keep a legacy route only when it preserves the needed data. | Carry one asymmetric checker through each individual export/import step. |
| Skin, bone or armor needs richer appearance | Hand paint, generate a regional source, or bake composed color onto the final UVs. | Compare color-only and deliberately shaded variants at game resolution. |
| A supported enhanced renderer should provide glow | Author a separate emissive source and renderer-specific material mapping. | Test a small bright core against both light and dark backgrounds. |

For current implementation, continue with [texture methods](textures-and-imagegen.md), [Blender export and baking](blender-export-and-baking.md), and the exact [compatibility profile](compatibility.md). These historical sources help diagnose a choice; they do not replace those renderer-specific instructions.

## Preserve useful motion and material identity

Shred18 explicitly says his replacement characters retain the original hierarchy and regular animations in **More JKR models**, 2006-01-26, post #33. That is direct author testimony supporting the stock-hierarchy route when familiar movement is desired. It does not mean a radically different body will avoid intersections or foot-placement problems. Preserve numbered nodes, pivots and intended rigid attachments, then inspect the changed proportions in motion. [Shred18's explanation](https://forums.massassi.net/Main_Massassi_Forums/Showcase/thread_38787_page_1.html)

For a weapon, use separate working examples for the external model and first-person model. EvanC's **Weapons: Part 1** (indexed 1998-08-07) demonstrates different offsets and hierarchy arrangements for those roles; its particular numeric values belong to its Bryar example. Z@NARDI's **Creating Internal Gun Models** (indexed 2000-01-31) reserves material slots for the hand in its supplied template. Its nine-texture allowance is a template layout, **not a universal engine material limit**. Modern assembly should remap indices systematically rather than copy those slot counts blindly. [EvanC](https://www.massassi.net/tutorials/weapon1/), [Z@NARDI](https://www.massassi.net/tutorials/gunmodels/)

GuNbOY's **Fixing Skin Textures** (indexed 1999-11-17) identifies a different failure from bad UVs: a torso added to a combined model still refers to the part's old material indices. Build a name-to-index mapping into the final material table and apply it to the imported faces; inspect each region afterward. Do not move UVs to compensate for a face using the wrong MAT. [GuNbOY's repair](https://www.massassi.net/tutorials/fixskin/)

The unresolved **MilkShape 3D problems with textures** report by Dbug454, 2014-01-28, #1, is a useful contrasting case: all TGA files appeared in the material list, but one image covered the combined group. File presence alone does not prove that material assignments survived conversion. The thread contains no demonstrated repair. [Report](https://forums.massassi.net/Editing_Forums/Jedi_Knight_and_Mysteries_of_the_Sith_Editing_Forum/thread_67431_page_1.html)

## Palette, transparency and MAT tool versions

In **Extracting Textures without pink flood?**, Xzero recommends the correct CMP on 2007-08-14 (#2/#6), and XDelusion reports corrected colors later that day (#8). Treat container extraction and palette-aware image decoding as separate operations. The historical tutorial author Red's **Finding Skin Palette Problems With JED** (indexed 1998-07-06) adds the player-skin concern: selected indexed colors can change with the environment's colormap. Keep the relevant CMP with indexed working inputs, and test the skin under representative palettes when that compatibility matters. [Thread](https://forums.massassi.net/Editing_Forums/Jedi_Knight_and_Mysteries_of_the_Sith_Editing_Forum/thread_48077_page_1.html), [Red's tutorial](https://www.massassi.net/tutorials/checking_skins/)

Do not turn one Mat16 version's behavior into a statement about every version. BoBo_Fett described **Mat16 1.2** as an incompletely tested beta with remembered memory-leak fixes on 2007-08-12 (#1). In a later conversion thread he described File-menu batch conversion in **1.3.1** on 2012-02-02 (#9); Dbug454 then reported input errors on 2012-02-04 (#10). That later claim corrects the older blanket claim that Mat16 has no batch operation, but does not prove every input works. Record the actual binary/version and test one known input before a batch. Only the tool posts from the otherwise level-oriented thread are used here. [1.2 author note](https://forums.massassi.net/Editing_Forums/Jedi_Knight_and_Mysteries_of_the_Sith_Editing_Forum/thread_48053_page_1.html), [1.3.1 author note and error report](https://forums.massassi.net/Editing_Forums/Jedi_Knight_and_Mysteries_of_the_Sith_Editing_Forum/thread_59981_page_1.html)

For unexpected holes, separate color-key conversion from face translucency. CaptBevvil's 2006-06-21 reply (#3) in **16 bit Mat Transparency Trouble** proposes that near-black detail became the transparency key during Mat16 conversion and suggests comparing JKPaint. The original reporter does not supply a confirmed result. Use an exact-black/near-black test strip, decode the converted output, and check which colors remain representable at its bit depth; compare the game result with the editor preview. [Historical converter diagnosis](https://forums.massassi.net/Editing_Forums/Jedi_Knight_and_Mysteries_of_the_Sith_Editing_Forum/thread_42110_page_1.html)

**MAT files - Solving the Unknown**, 2005-04-04–05, contains an initial header hypothesis from Jon`C, counterexperiments from Sige (#3–5), and a header description from BoBo_Fett (#6). Use it as a record of format investigation, including its corrections. Changing a bit-depth number in a header does not establish that the loader supports a new pixel format. Distinguish animation cells from the mip chain within an image. For a current conversion, inspect decoded pixels and supported formats rather than copying experimental header edits. [Format discussion](https://forums.massassi.net/Editing_Forums/Jedi_Knight_and_Mysteries_of_the_Sith_Editing_Forum/thread_31176_page_1.html)

## UVs, mipmaps and geometry round trips

In **Importing from milkshape to JOKE and JED**, darthslaw reports UV damage even at unit scaling on 2006-01-31 (#4), contradicting the earlier power-of-two scaling suggestion. Ruthven later identifies a 256-square texture assumption in the old MilkShape exporter on 2006-02-11 (#9); the original poster acknowledges using 64-square inputs but does not report a completed successful retest. This is an exporter-specific diagnostic, not a requirement that every JK texture be 256 square. [Contested conversion advice](https://forums.massassi.net/Editing_Forums/Jedi_Knight_and_Mysteries_of_the_Sith_Editing_Forum/thread_39041_page_1.html)

**Texturing small 3dos** supplies another counterexample: after a scale-up/texture/scale-down workaround was proposed, eggtooth reported UV damage from JED transforms on 2005-10-28 (#8) and said JOKE preserved scaling in that case. The practical choice is explicit UV editing with numeric before/after checks, not confidence in a round trip because both tools display the mesh. [Discussion](https://forums.massassi.net/Editing_Forums/Jedi_Knight_and_Mysteries_of_the_Sith_Editing_Forum/thread_36773_page_1.html)

Large textures require renderer-specific UV calibration. **NEED MAJOR TESTING 512x512 mats in JK**, 2005-07-13–15, records mixed results across old hardware; ProdigyOddigy distinguishes world-surface alignment from 3DO alignment (#6), while Sige reports wrapping/clipping problems (#8/#11). Do not import that experiment's texture-size setting or UV division as a universal model fix. Use the current [UV-period procedure](textures-and-imagegen.md): an asymmetric checker, known UV span, near/far views and partially offscreen views on the exact renderer. This remains compatible with the separately audited native-GL denominator clamp. [Historical tests](https://forums.massassi.net/Editing_Forums/Jedi_Knight_and_Mysteries_of_the_Sith_Editing_Forum/thread_34115_page_1.html)

Keep mip testing separate from geometric LOD testing. In **3do LOD**, eggtooth reports the low-detail geoset flashing at close range on 2005-10-11/19; the thread never establishes a fix. Preserve all geosets and inspect their material references, visibility and transitions rather than accepting only the close mesh's export. [Unresolved LOD report](https://forums.massassi.net/Editing_Forums/Jedi_Knight_and_Mysteries_of_the_Sith_Editing_Forum/thread_36447_page_1.html)

A `.3do` suffix is also insufficient format identification: F-Body distinguishes Jedi Knight and Total Annihilation 3DO formats in **Converting from .3DS to .3DO?**, 2005-08-24 (#2). Verify the Sith structure and converted materials. Sige's firsthand plugin discussion on 2006-08-13 (#4) separately explains the mismatch between MilkShape bone assignments and KEY's rigid mesh motion; the proposed converter in that thread was not demonstrated. Preserve the target hierarchy when using an animation proxy. [Format distinction](https://forums.massassi.net/Editing_Forums/Jedi_Knight_and_Mysteries_of_the_Sith_Editing_Forum/thread_35250_page_1.html), [Plugin discussion](https://forums.massassi.net/Editing_Forums/Jedi_Knight_and_Mysteries_of_the_Sith_Editing_Forum/thread_43094_page_1.html)

Blender-Sith has its own version history. Brian's public news post on 2022-05-26 attributes an announcement to Crt Vavros: the Blender 2.79-era beta had batch import checks for JK/MotS, less export testing, and help from Shred testing some exports in-game. That supports crediting the author and tester while keeping import coverage separate from export proof. This is a public repost of a Discord statement, not a direct Discord inspection, and not a guarantee for Blender 5.x. Use the current [fork audit](blender-sith-fork.md) for the actual supported changes. [Public announcement](https://www.massassi.net/news_archive_2022-5.html)

## Shading failures: inspect data before repainting

For a missing back side on a deliberately thin cape or similar part, inspect face sidedness. SavageX378 and Ruthven correct the opening replies in **double sided mats**, 2006-01-04 (#3/#4): the relevant setting is on the face, with Ruthven identifying `0x1` and warning of round-trip loss. This is different from MAT transparency. Confirm the exported flag instead of doubling every face to hide a winding problem. [Corrected advice](https://forums.massassi.net/Editing_Forums/Jedi_Knight_and_Mysteries_of_the_Sith_Editing_Forum/thread_38476_page_1.html)

An invalid normal can instead originate in invalid geometry. **Incorrect normal - surface must be invalid!** starts with a collapsed cone face on 2002-07-31; Descent_pilot recommends removing the degenerate face (#3). Alco's 2014-08-24 follow-up (#5) warns that a recalculation can remove the error message without repairing the surface. This source concerns JED construction; the transferable model diagnostic is zero-area/duplicate vertices and winding before normal edits. [Discussion](https://forums.massassi.net/Editing_Forums/Jedi_Knight_and_Mysteries_of_the_Sith_Editing_Forum/thread_10609_page_1.html)

**Fully Lit 3do**, 2006-06-13–17, mixes distinct possibilities. Ruthven and F-Body point to exported extra-light values (#3/#8); darthslaw explicitly labels a duplicate-texture-vertex limit explanation as untested (#6). Inspect actual face/mesh lighting fields and compare against a known-working file. Do not present the guessed resource limit or repeated resaving as an established fix. [Lighting investigation](https://forums.massassi.net/Editing_Forums/Jedi_Knight_and_Mysteries_of_the_Sith_Editing_Forum/thread_41914_page_1.html)

There are stronger bounded reports: Jim14 confirms MikeC's geometry-mode change for a **color-only MAT** on a projectile, 2001-04-18 (#6). That does not authorize changing every textured face from mode 4 to mode 3. SG-fan's **artifact/hom 3do errors** instead affects stock and custom models and is reportedly removed by changing viewport size, 2006-06-17 (#10). When the stock control fails too, investigate the renderer/driver configuration before remaking the model. Neither historical result was rerun for this research. [Color-only projectile case](https://forums.massassi.net/Editing_Forums/Jedi_Knight_and_Mysteries_of_the_Sith_Editing_Forum/thread_7715_page_1.html), [Stock-control case](https://forums.massassi.net/Editing_Forums/Jedi_Knight_and_Mysteries_of_the_Sith_Editing_Forum/thread_41968_page_1.html)

## Painted material, baked detail, and enhanced glow

Shred's work is useful partly because the discussion records corrections. In **Latest from JKE**, Shred18 rejects the assumed noise filter on 2006-07-24 (#24), suggesting that the criticism probably refers to bump-map or brush work he used. Preserve that qualification and the distinction between a noisy appearance and an inferred production technique. Compare reduced-size model renders and simplify distracting detail regardless of which tool produced it. The post does **not** prove that retail JK rendered a runtime normal map. [Author correction](https://forums.massassi.net/Main_Massassi_Forums/Showcase/thread_42777_page_1.html)

In **Texture Dump!**, Shred18 describes companion normal maps on 2009-09-08 (#18); RogerSpruce and Jon`C criticize inconsistent lighting in the color sources (#12/#21). This was surface-art discussion, not a documented JK character bake. Its transferable lesson is to choose whether a source is intrinsic color or intentionally shaded artwork before mapping it to a moving character. A companion normal map does not remove light already painted into color. No linked image was visually audited here. [Material discussion](https://forums.massassi.net/Main_Massassi_Forums/Showcase/thread_55304_page_1.html)

Mip seams deserve an atlas test of their own. On 2022-06-01, Quib Mask reports having seen seams on JKE models and **hypothesizes** that unused UV-gap colors bleed into visible pixels during mip generation (#10). Inspect lower mip levels and compare an edge-dilated atlas against the original; this is a sensible experiment, not a proven fix from that post. The same post explicitly distinguishes generated normal-map data from what JKGM then consumed; Jon`C's follow-up (#11) discusses possible future uses. Do not promote that future possibility into a shipped feature. [Quadranscentennial thread, page 1](https://forums.massassi.net/Editing_Forums/Jedi_Knight_and_Mysteries_of_the_Sith_Editing_Forum/thread_68242_page_1.html)

For an enhanced glow route, **Anything games**, page 27, distinguishes alpha blending, emissive output and illumination of nearby geometry. Jon`C advises alpha preparation on 2019-03-19 (#1047). SMLiberator recommends a restrained emissive core on 2019-03-21 (#1079); let the renderer provide the halo. Nikumubeki's cell-index error was explicitly resolved (#1059–62), and his later resource-path correction (#1075) shows why compilation must read the intended MATs. This is jkgfxmod-era advice, not native retail support. Use the existing [JKGM/PNG route](textures-and-imagegen.md) and its versioned mapping; test every animation cell and the actual runtime. [Developer and author exchange](https://forums.massassi.net/Main_Massassi_Forums/Discussion_Forum/thread_67886_page_27.html)

The broader search did not establish a reproducible community high-to-low character-bake recipe or a new universal Gouraud/vertex-normal rule. Use the documented [baking options](blender-export-and-baking.md) for that work. Keep procedural geometry conversion, color baking, high-to-low detail transfer and game lighting distinct. A generated or sculpted donor still needs deliberate low-mesh silhouette, openings, UVs and articulation.

## What was actually read and retained

The [machine-readable source ledger](evidence/community-surface-sources.json) records exact URLs, authors, post dates/numbers, source classifications, unresolved points, retrieval byte counts/hashes, and exact supplemental queries. Dates on forum posts retain the archive's displayed calendar date; their timezone was not inferred. Tutorial dates come from Massassi's tutorial index, not an invented page timestamp.

Discovery included the JK/MotS editing index, three Showcase index pages, the tutorial index, and searches using **3do, model, skin, MAT, palette, UV, vertices, normals, Gouraud, lightingmode, mipmaps, mip maps, mipmapping, bake, baking, Shred18, MilkShape**. Some searches returned only architecture, unrelated games, proposals or empty requests; those do not become model guidance. This is a bounded research pass, not a claim to have exhausted the archive.

Public files contain original summaries and source metadata. Full thread bodies, artwork, attachments, example stock assets and personal contact details are not redistributed. Public availability or a permission given to one forum participant is not a general license to ship their model or texture. The private capture records document reading, not authorship rights or reproduced game behavior. No GPU generation, historical converter execution, game launch or multiplayer test occurred during this research pass.
